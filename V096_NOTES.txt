HP20 v0.9.6 RC1 - WHAT CHANGED
==============================

USER UX
- Open setup AP: no temporary HP20 password.
- Captive portal endpoints for Android/iOS/Windows.
- Nearby Wi-Fi sorted by signal with human-readable quality labels.
- Main screen only asks for Wi-Fi + password.
- ThingsBoard/token/CA/telemetry/OTA moved to Advanced.
- Save page immediately shows connection progress.

DEVICE FEEDBACK
- 1 beep + fast blue blink: connecting after Save.
- 2 beeps + short solid blue: connected.
- 3 beeps + triple pulse: not connected after ~20 s.
- Existing green thermal-comfort LED semantics remain unchanged.

NETWORK/SECURITY
- Open AP is gated by first-boot or physical BOOT hold ~3 s (Serial SETUP also available).
- Ordinary Wi-Fi loss never auto-opens the setup AP.
- Portal timeout remains 10 minutes.
- Credentials stay in NVS; real secrets.h is excluded from this package.

THINGSBOARD
- Standard telemetry cadence: 5 minutes.
- Legacy 15-minute baseline is migrated once to 5 minutes.
- Initial/onboarding validation may send earlier; normal cadence resumes afterward.

OTA
- OTA remains opt-in/off by default.
- OTA polling choices: 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 3 h, 6 h, 12 h, 24 h.
- New config default: 1 h.
- Existing HTTPS + title/version + SHA-256 gates preserved.
